# Maven Market Sales Analysis

SQL-based sales data analysis using PostgreSQL to uncover revenue trends, growth patterns, and product performance.

## Dataset
Maven Market sales data with business questions solved using SQL.

## Queries Covered
- Q: Monthly Revenue Trend
- Q: Yearly Revenue
- Q: Year-on-Year Growth
- Q: Month with Highest Revenue 
- Q: Month with Highest Revenue 
- Q: Average Monthly Revenue
- Q: Top 5 Products by Revenue
- Q: Number of New And Repeater Customer
- Q: Revenue by Product Category
- Q: TOP 3 product per category
- Q: Revenue by Store
- Q: Month-on-Month Growth %

## Tools Used
- PostgreSQL
- GitHub

## Key Insights
- Identified peak revenue months and growth trends
- Analyzed product and category-wise performance
